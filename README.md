### 🚀 What I Do

I'm someone who loves taking **Kotlin** for a spin to bring random ideas to life. My favorite playground? **Background features & system functions.** 

You know those processes running quietly under the hood that nobody pays attention to? I like to pull them into the spotlight, wrap them in a UI, and showcase what kind of fun (or silly, or surprisingly useful) applications we can build with them. 🛠️⚡
